Install on iPhone / iPad
  1. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Supplybot is on your home screen.
One more step: open index.html and paste Supplybot’s address into the APP_URL line (see README.md).
